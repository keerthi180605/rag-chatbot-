2. Abstract
A brief overview (around 200–300 words) summarizing the purpose, features, and impact of EduPlay.

Abstract Example:
EduPlay is a web-based quiz game platform designed to blend education with entertainment. By providing users with engaging, interactive quizzes across a range of topics, EduPlay enhances learning retention and promotes self-paced study. The platform targets students, educators, and lifelong learners, offering features such as timed quizzes, instant feedback, leaderboards, and progress tracking. Built with modern web technologies, EduPlay emphasizes user experience, accessibility, and content variety. This project explores the concept, design process, implementation, challenges faced, and future scope of EduPlay.
